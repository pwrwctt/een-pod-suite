# Core EEN terminology

Source basis: Enterprise Europe Network Glossary 2024 v3.

Use EEN terminology consistently.

- **Partnering profile:** a cooperation request or offer outlining the client, partner sought and expected cooperation; types BO, BR, TO, TR, RDR.
- **POD — Partnering Opportunities Database:** database of business, technology and research cooperation offers/requests.
- **Partnering Services:** international brokerage/matchmaking services supporting business/commercial, technology/knowledge-transfer and R&D collaborations.
- **Technology transfer:** successful application or adaptation of an innovative technology from one organisation or industrial sector to another.
- **EEN Community:** the Network IT platform; COMMUNITY supports day-to-day client/service work and contains client cards, action plans and partnering opportunities.
- **Client Card:** record holding client information and service history.
- **Action Plan:** agreed concrete actions/touchpoints arising from the needs assessment and registered in EEN Community.
- **Hub:** the Network entity/adviser acting as case manager and main client contact.
- **Network advisor:** staff member of a Network host organisation providing value-added advisory and partnering services.
- **Network effect:** added value generated by sharing information, resources and competences across Network partners.
- **Partnering Achievement:** significant positive outcome from partnering services, typically a concrete medium- to long-term collaboration with measurable/identifiable impact.

Do not substitute generic CRM/project terminology when an established EEN term applies.
