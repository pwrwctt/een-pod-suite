# POD operational form-readiness schema

Partnering Profile Quality Guidelines v1.2 remain the primary source for profile quality and mandatory content. The supplied BO/BR/TO/TR Cooperation Profile Templates are used only for operational form limits and available controls.

## Confirmed limits for BO / BR / TO / TR

| Field | Maximum characters |
|---|---:|
| Profile Title | 256 |
| Short Summary | 500 |
| Full Description | 4000 |
| Expected role of partner | 4000 |

Additional limits:

| Field | BO | BR | TO | TR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Advantages and Innovation | 2000 | 2000, optional | 2000 | 2000, optional |
| Technical Specification / Expertise Sought | — | 2000 | — | 2000 |

## Mandatory versus available

A visible form control is not automatically mandatory.

The templates show Stage of development, IPR Status, SDG, Market keywords, Technology keywords, Targeted countries, Sector groups involved, Media and Type/size of partner.

Use PPQG v1.2 for mandatory status:
- TO: Stage of development and IPR are required.
- TR: Stage/IPR may add value but are not mandatory.
- BO/BR: Stage/IPR are available controls but not core mandatory checklist fields.

## Type and size of partner values shown in templates

- SME <=10
- SME 11-49
- SME 50-249
- Big company
- R&D Institution
- University
- Other

Treat these as template-era values; verify live POD values when currency matters.

## Dissemination controls shown

- Market keywords — up to 5
- Technology keywords — up to 5
- Targeted countries
- Sector groups involved
- Media: Image / PDF / Video Pitch

Do not make Sector Groups or Media mandatory solely because controls exist.

## Review behaviour

For BO/BR/TO/TR, report exact counts for populated limited fields:
- PASS
- OVER LIMIT by N
- OPTIONAL / EMPTY
- NOT APPLICABLE

Never silently truncate content.

## Template inconsistency

The Business Request template contains `product/service on offer` in one Full Description instruction. For BR use **product/service requested**, following PPQG v1.2.
