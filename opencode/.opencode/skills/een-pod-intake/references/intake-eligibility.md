# Intake, eligibility and groundwork

Source basis: Partnering Profile Quality Guidelines v1.2, sections 2.2–2.7 and 3.1.

## Eligibility gate

Before drafting, establish the client type.

- SMEs are the primary POD users.
- PPQG v1.2 acknowledges that some technology transfers and licence agreements need not be long-term; do not reject a genuine transfer solely because its duration is short.
- A large business (non-SME) may publish **BR** and **TR** only when the profile enables international activities for European SMEs.
- A large business (non-SME) may not publish **BO** or **TO**.
- Universities and research centres may publish any profile type.
- Export consultants are prohibited from using POD to publish profiles.

## Profiles not allowed

Reject or redirect profiles whose purpose is:
- direct sale of products or services;
- illegal activities or weapons;
- offensive content.

A BO must be a medium- to long-term partnering opportunity, not a simple search for customers.

## Groundwork before drafting

Confirm:
1. a needs assessment has identified a concrete international cooperation need;
2. the client is committed and has the resources/skills to pursue cooperation;
3. the cooperation fits a realistic internationalisation strategy;
4. the Client Card exists;
5. an Action Plan has been agreed and registered;
6. existing POD profiles have been checked first for a suitable match.

## Ownership / operational gate

- The profile creator should submit profiles only for clients in the geographical area covered by their organisation.
- Only the **Hub** that entered the Client Card can draft the POD profile for that client.

When the user only asks for text drafting, do not pretend that these operational steps have been completed. Mark them as `OPERATIONAL CHECK` if evidence is missing.
