# Partnership types

Source basis: Partnering Profile Quality Guidelines v1.2, Annex II and section 6.2.2.

## Selection rule

Select the most accurate partnership type(s).
The Guidelines recommend no more than **1–3** types to avoid an unfocused search.
The selected types must:
- appear in the Short Summary;
- be explained in the Full Description;
- match the Expected Role of Partner.

## Business profiles — BO / BR

Allowed types in v1.2:
- **Commercial agreement**
- **Investment agreement**
- **Outsourcing agreement**
- **Supplier agreement**

### Commercial agreement
Medium- to long-term collaboration in buying/selling goods/services.
Examples include agents, distributors, franchise arrangements, subcontracting and licence-related business cooperation, depending on offer/request direction.

### Investment agreement
Capital investment, loan, acquisition of shares or a genuine joint venture creating a new business entity.

### Outsourcing agreement
A business process is contracted to a service provider; may include manufacturing.

### Supplier agreement
Long-term supply of goods/services.

## Technology profiles — TO / TR

Allowed types in v1.2:
- **Investment agreement**
- **Commercial agreement with technical assistance**
- **Research & development cooperation agreement**

### Commercial agreement with technical assistance
Non-exclusive acquisition of a product/technology combined with services essential to technology transfer; may include licensing, innovative manufacturing or transfer support/training.

### Investment agreement
Investment in a technology project/business, including a genuine joint venture where appropriate.

### Research & development cooperation agreement
Two entities pool R&D capacities for exploratory development of a new solution/technology.

## RDR

Only:
- **Research & development cooperation agreement**

Do not use a generic label without describing the partner's concrete tasks.
