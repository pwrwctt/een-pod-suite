# POD field matrix and hard constraints

Source basis: Partnering Profile Quality Guidelines v1.2, sections 5 and 6.2.

## Cross-profile fields

All profile types require:
- Profile Type;
- Profile Owner;
- Client Company linkage;
- Title;
- Short Summary;
- Full Description;
- Sustainable Development Goal selection;
- Expected role of partner;
- Type of Partnership;
- Type and size of partner;
- Market keywords;
- appropriate dissemination / target-country choices.

**Short Summary limit:** 500 characters.

**Partnership types:** choose the most accurate options. More than one is possible, but the Guidelines recommend **no more than 1–3**, otherwise the search may appear unfocused.

## BO — Business Offer

Required/expected content:
- Title: product/service offered + key cooperation type; country recommended.
- Summary: country + field + offer + selected cooperation type(s).
- Description: client background + offer features/application + WHY/HOW cooperation.
- Advantages & Innovations: mandatory.
- Market keywords: max 5.
- Partner sought: exact role.
- Partnership type: accurate and coherent.

Technology keywords may be used for BO if they add value and are relevant.

## BR — Business Request

Required/expected content:
- Title: product/service requested + key cooperation type; country recommended.
- Summary: country + field + request + selected cooperation type(s).
- Description: request + features/application + WHY/HOW cooperation.
- Technical specification / expertise sought: mandatory.
- Market keywords: max 5.
- Partner sought + accurate partnership type.

Technology keywords may be used for BR if they add value and are relevant.

## TO — Technology Offer

Required/expected content:
- Title: technology/know-how offered, self-explanatory for non-experts.
- Summary: organisation type + country + field + technology + main advantage/application + cooperation type(s).
- Description: problem/state of art + technology + application + WHY/HOW cooperation.
- Advantages & Innovations: mandatory.
- Stage of development: mandatory.
- IPR status: mandatory.
- Technology keywords: mandatory, max 5.
- Market keywords: max 5.
- Partner role + accurate partnership type.

Allowed stage options in the 2024 guidance:
- Concept stage
- Under development
- Lab tested
- Available for demonstration
- Already on the market

Allowed IPR options in the 2024 guidance:
- No IPR applied
- Secret know-how
- IPR applied but not yet granted
- IPR granted

If anonymity is required, do not disclose patent/design/trademark numbers.

## TR — Technology Request

Required/expected content:
- Title: technology/know-how requested.
- Summary: organisation type + country + field + technology needed + why + cooperation type(s).
- Description: why technology is needed + requirements + application + WHY/HOW cooperation.
- Technical specification & know-how: mandatory.
- Technology keywords: mandatory, max 5.
- Market keywords: max 5.
- Partner role + accurate partnership type.
- Stage/IPR can add value but are not mandatory in v1.2.

## RDR — Research & Development Request

Checklist content:
- Title: funding programme/framework + targeted partner type/role.
- Summary: organisation/country + project objective + programme/call + partner expertise/role.
- Description: project context/objective + programme conditions + consortium/partner role + timescales.
- Technology keywords: max 5.
- Market keywords: max 5.
- Partner sought: exact type and role.
- Type of Partnership: Research & development cooperation agreement.

RDR call details include:
- Framework Program: mandatory.
- Call title and identifier: mandatory.
- Coordinator required (Yes/No): mandatory.
- Deadline for EOIs: mandatory.
- Deadline for Call: mandatory.
- Submission/evaluation scheme: provide when relevant.
- Anticipated project budget: useful.
- Project duration in weeks: useful.
- Web link to call: if applicable.
- Project title/acronym: if known.

Leave enough time between EOI deadline and call deadline.

## SDG

SDG is a mandatory field in v1.2. Select one or more applicable SDGs from the available list; choose **Not Relevant** if none is appropriate.
