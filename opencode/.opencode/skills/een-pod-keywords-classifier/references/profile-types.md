# Profile type classification: BO / BR / TO / TR / RDR

Source basis: Partnering Profile Quality Guidelines v1.2, Annex I section 6.1; EEN Glossary 2024 v3.

## Decision sequence

### 1. Business or Technology?

Choose a **Technology** profile when:
- a specific innovative technology, process or know-how is offered/requested; and
- technology transfer is expected.

Technology transfer means successful application/adaptation of an innovative technology from one organisation/sector to another.

Choose a **Business** profile when the cooperation mainly concerns:
- existing products/services or mainstream technologies;
- trade intermediaries or suppliers;
- franchise arrangements;
- business partnerships without technology transfer.

### 2. Offer or Request?

**BO — Business Offer**
Use when the client offers products/services on external markets through medium- or long-term cooperation, e.g. commercial representation/distribution, outsourcing, manufacturing, investment or other allowed business partnership.

**BR — Business Request**
Use when the client requests products/services, suppliers, products to complement a range, investment opportunities or similar business inputs.

**TO — Technology Offer**
Use for a **specific innovative technology, process or know-how** made available to a partner.
Do not use TO for:
- a generic offer of expertise/capability;
- direct sale of a product/service.

**TR — Technology Request**
Use when the client seeks a technology, process or specific know-how from a prospective partner.

**RDR — Research & Development Request**
Use only for a concrete R&D partner search linked to:
- a specific EU-funded research call; or
- a specific bilateral call, with at least one partner in an EU Member State or SMP-associated country.

Only project partners/coordinators already involved in a consortium and preparing a response to a call may use RDR to search for partners.
There is no R&D Offer profile.

## Confidence rule

If the evidence does not establish business-vs-technology transfer or offer-vs-request direction, return:
- recommended type;
- confidence: high / medium / low;
- decisive missing question(s).

Do not force a type merely to proceed with drafting.
