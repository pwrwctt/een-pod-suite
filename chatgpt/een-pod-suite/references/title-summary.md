# Title and Short Summary optimisation

Source basis: Partnering Profile Quality Guidelines v1.2, sections 5 and 6.2.1.

## Title

Write a title that is:
- clear and concise;
- explicit enough for an adviser/client to decide whether to read further;
- free of spelling errors, specific unexplained acronyms, marketing language and unnecessary capitalisation.

Do not use ALL CAPS or capitalise every word.

### BO / BR
Include:
- product/service offered or requested;
- key cooperation type;
- country is recommended where useful.

### TO / TR
Highlight the specific technology/know-how offered/requested in terms understandable to non-experts.

### RDR
Make the sought partner obvious and mention the funding programme/framework.

## Short Summary

Hard limit: **500 characters**.

Do not copy:
- the title; or
- the first paragraph of the description.

Title and Summary may be the only parts initially disseminated to clients, so make them a complete preview of the opportunity.

### BO / BR summary
Include:
- country;
- client field/sector;
- product/service offered/requested and use;
- cooperation type(s);
- a concise advantage if space permits.

### TO summary
Include:
- organisation type + country + field;
- technology/know-how;
- application;
- main advantage/innovation;
- stage when relevant;
- cooperation type(s).

### TR summary
Include:
- organisation type + country + field;
- technology sought;
- key requirements;
- intended use;
- required maturity where relevant;
- cooperation type(s).

### RDR summary
Include:
- coordinator/partner type + geography;
- project objective;
- programme/call;
- partner expertise and role.

If dissemination is intentionally restricted to only a few countries, mention that in the summary where useful.

## Optimisation gate

Return:
- recommended Title;
- character-counted Short Summary (`N/500 characters`);
- up to two alternatives only when they offer materially different matching strategies;
- unresolved `MISSING INPUT` separately.
